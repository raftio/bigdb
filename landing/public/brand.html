LOCKUP — ON LIGHT

Mark is a 2×2 bit matrix on a 16-unit grid: 7×7 cells, 2 gap, 1 corner radius. The diagonal is set, the anti-diagonal is not — the smallest thing that still reads as a bitmap. Clear space equals one cell (7 units). Never recolour the unset cells to the accent; that removes the only idea in the mark.
